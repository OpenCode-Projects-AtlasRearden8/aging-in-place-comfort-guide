# The Walk-Through Checklist

*Print this, grab a pen, and do the [Room-by-Room Walkthrough](room-by-room.md) with it in hand. Check your reality, not the brochure version of your home.*

**Ground rules for the walk:**

- Wear the shoes you actually putter around in.
- Follow your real daily route: bed → bathroom → kitchen → living room → door.
- Mark items: ✅ good · ❌ fix it · ⚠️ workable, note the details.
- At the end, circle the **worst three** ❌s. Those are Dose 1–3 on the [Weekend Doses](weekend-fall-fixes.md) plan.

---

## Getting to the door

- [ ] Walkway/steps solid and even (no broken lips or wobble)
- [ ] Handrail on entry steps, tight enough to lean on
- [ ] Exterior light at each door, motion or dusk-to-dawn
- [ ] Door opens without wrestling; keyhole reachable
- [ ] Bench/table near the door for parcels & groceries

## Hallways and walking routes (bed→bath→kitchen)

- [ ] No loose rugs on any walking path
- [ ] No cords, shoes, or clutter in lanes
- [ ] Night lights cover the whole 3 a.m. route
- [ ] 32-inch clear width throughout

## Living room

- [ ] No stepladder needed for daily items (shelves at waist–eye height)
- [ ] Seating: hips level-with-or-above knees in the two main chairs
- [ ] Armrests on main chairs; sturdy side table nearby
- [ ] Cords taped/covered along the wall
- [ ] Remote, phone, glasses have a fixed "home"

## Kitchen

- [ ] Daily dishes/glasses at waist–shoulder height
- [ ] Heavy items moved down to mid shelves
- [ ] Landing surface next to oven (counter or cart)
- [ ] Under-cabinet or counter lighting at work stations
- [ ] Smoke detector present and tested (press the button, today)
- [ ] Water detector under sink (or on list)

## Bathroom

- [ ] Grab bar(s) beside toilet — into studs, not towel bars
- [ ] Grab bar(s) in tub/shower — entry side + back wall
- [ ] Non-slip surface inside tub/shower
- [ ] Toilet height OK (hips ~level with knees), or seat raiser in place
- [ ] Toiletries reachable seated or standing — never floor-of-tub level
- [ ] Hand-held shower head or plans for one

## Bedroom

- [ ] Bed: hips level with knees sitting on the edge
- [ ] Phone and charger within arm's reach of pillow
- [ ] Lamp reachable from bed without stretching
- [ ] Clear turn-around space beside the bed
- [ ] Daily clothes in the easy two-thirds of the closet

## Stairs (inside and out)

- [ ] Handrails top AND bottom, tight when pulled
- [ ] Steps visible: contrast edge on dark treads
- [ ] Lights at both ends; switch reachable top and bottom
- [ ] Nothing "dropped to take upstairs later" living on the steps

## Medicine & appointments

- [ ] One home base for all medications
- [ ] Weekly organizer in use; fill day on the calendar
- [ ] One-page med list current (copy in wallet)
- [ ] One calendar holding all appointments; capture habit in place
- [ ] Phone has volume-on reminders (test one now)

## Emergency basics

- [ ] Smoke detectors every level; CO detector if fuel-burning anything
- [ ] Test button pressed on all of them today
- [ ] Flashlight within reach of bed, working batteries
- [ ] Emergency contacts + med list taped to fridge
- [ ] Phone numbers readable (big font saved list in phone)
- [ ] Who has a house key? (Answer written down and shared)
