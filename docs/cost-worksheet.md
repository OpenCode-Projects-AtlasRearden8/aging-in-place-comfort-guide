# Upgrade-Cost Worksheet

*Budget planning help only — this is a worksheet, not insurance, financial, or investment advice. Prices are typical US ranges rounded for planning; your region, your quality tier, and your handyman will differ. Fill in real quotes.*

How to use: pick your projects from the other pages, look up (or get quotes for) local prices, and write them in the shouty-big blanks. Then compare against the "stay home" column of the [Assisted Living comparison table](assisted-living.md).

## Step 4 — Totals and the only sentence that matters

- **One-time total (A + B): $ ____________**
- **Year-one total (A + B + C): $ ____________**
- **Year-two-plus total (C, plus maintenance): $ ____________**

The only sentence that matters:

> "For $ ____________ a year, I can keep living in my own home safely. Compared to the assisted-living base rates we researched at $ ____________ a month, year one of staying home costs the same as ______ months of assisted living."

Notice what that sentence is: not a verdict. Just numbers next to each other, which is exactly what a family meeting needs. Now bring it to the [family conversation](family-conversations.md) — numbers are much easier to agree on than adjectives.
