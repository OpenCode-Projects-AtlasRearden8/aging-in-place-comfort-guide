The Walk-Through Checklist

Print this, grab a pen, and do the Room-by-Room Walkthrough with it in hand. Check your reality, not the brochure version of your home.

Ground rules for the walk:

  • Wear the shoes you actually putter around in.
  • Follow your real daily route: bed → bathroom → kitchen → living room → door.
  • Mark items: ✅ good · ❌ fix it · ⚠️ workable, note the details.
  • At the end, circle the worst three ❌s. Those are Dose 1–3 on the Weekend Doses plan.
